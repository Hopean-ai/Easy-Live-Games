CFO Career

From startup to public company, as its CFO.

CFO Career keeps your career on your account, so you can stop and pick it up later.

Sign in or create an account to start.

Sam has a widget shop, one machine, and big plans. You are its first CFO. Sam runs the shop; the money and the books are yours.

Each turn is a week. The shop's receipts and bills land on your desk, and you post them as journal entries. At each month end you file the books, and an accountant reviews them. You have 30 hours a week, and the paperwork grows with the business, so sooner or later you'll hand the routine work to a point-of-sale system or a bookkeeper.

When Sam wants to grow, you decide how to pay for it. When Sam wants to cut corners with the tax office, you decide that too. At the end of the year the company needs strong revenue and clean books to be ready for the next stage.
