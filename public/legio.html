Legio

From recruit to emperor, if you live.

Legio keeps your career on your account, so you can stop and pick it up later, and puts your best career on the leaderboard.

Sign in or create an account to enlist.

It is 235 AD. The army has just murdered its emperor and hailed one of its own, a soldier who rose from the ranks. For the next fifty years, emperors will be made and killed by the legions.

You enlist as a recruit. Every half-year you choose what to do; battles, plague, patrons and civil wars do the rest. Climb from tiro to centurio, to primus pilus, and perhaps to the purple. You have one life, and a full career takes about an hour.
